## About the Game

Attritable Swarm is a browser-based tactical arcade game inspired by modern military concepts of attritable systems and drone swarms.

You command a mobile drone carrier positioned along the forward line. Incoming targets advance toward your position while you decide when and where to commit your limited drone inventory.

Larger and more heavily protected targets require larger swarms and more time to neutralize.

The longer you remain in the fight, the more dangerous the battlefield becomes.

Manage your arsenal carefully, destroy incoming threats, build your score, and see how far you can advance.

---

## How to Play

### Move

Move your drone carrier between battlefield lanes.

- **A / Left Arrow** — Move left
- **D / Right Arrow** — Move right

### Deploy

Select the number of drones you want to commit to a target.

Then release the swarm against a target in your lane.

- **Space** — Release swarm
- **Mouse** — Select targets and interact with the game
- **Touch controls** — Available on supported devices

### Survive

Targets become increasingly difficult to neutralize.

Larger and hardened targets require more drones and additional time to destroy.

Targets that are not eliminated can strike back.

---

## Game Mechanics

### Drone Arsenal

Your available drone inventory is finite.

Launching larger swarms gives you more destructive capability, but consumes more of your available arsenal.

Your arsenal gradually replenishes over time.

Depleting your arsenal completely can end the mission.

### Targets

Different battlefield targets require different levels of swarm commitment.

Choose carefully when deciding how many drones to deploy.

### Score

Destroying targets contributes to your score.

Efficient swarm employment can improve your performance and help you climb through the game's military rank system.

### Progression

Survive as many levels as possible while the battlefield becomes progressively more difficult.

## The Concept

The game takes inspiration from the idea of **attritable systems** — relatively low-cost unmanned systems that can be deployed in significant numbers when compared with traditional high-value platforms.

The game turns that concept into a simple tactical decision:

**How many drones are you willing to commit to the target in front of you?**

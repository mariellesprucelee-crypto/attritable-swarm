FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

IS ATTRITABLE SWARM FREE?

Yes. The game is completely free to play and is supported by advertising from Google AdSense.

DO I NEED TO DOWNLOAD ANYTHING OR CREATE AN ACCOUNT?

No. It runs in your browser with no download, installation, or sign-up.

WHAT DEVICES AND BROWSERS WORK?

Any modern desktop or mobile browser. Play with a keyboard and mouse, or with the on-screen touch controls on phones and tablets.

WHAT ARE THE CONTROLS?

  • A / D or the arrow keys: move between lanes
  • Space or the RELEASE button: release the swarm on the target in your lane
  • Click or tap a target: fire on it directly
  • P: pause   H or ?: field manual   M: mute

IS MY PROGRESS SAVED?

Your best score, callsign, and sound setting are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. Clearing site data, using private browsing, or switching browsers or devices can reset them.

HOW DOES A MISSION END?

A mission ends when your health reaches zero, or when your arsenal reaches zero and your last swarm has landed. Cleared levels restore some health and raise your arsenal cap.

WHAT DOES "ATTRITABLE" MEAN?

Attritable systems are relatively low-cost and expendable, so they can be used in large numbers instead of risking a few expensive platforms. The game turns that idea into a simple decision: how many drones will you commit to the target in front of you?

HOW DO RANKS WORK?

Your rank is based on your score, starting at Second Lieutenant and rising through the officer ladder to General of the Army at 84,000 points. Your rank appears in the top-left panel and on your shared score card.

WHAT IS THE RED "NEED" MARKER ABOVE THE SLIDER?

It shows how many drones are needed to finish your current or hovered target at its present health. See the strategy guide for tips on using it.

WHY DID THE GAME PAUSE WHEN I SWITCHED TABS?

The game pauses automatically when the tab or window loses focus so you never lose a run while away. Press the resume button or P to continue.

HOW DO I SHARE MY SCORE?

On the mission-end screen, press SHARE SCORE. You can use your device's share sheet, post to X/Twitter, Facebook, WhatsApp, or Reddit, copy the text, or download a score card image.

IS THE GAME AFFILIATED WITH THE U.S. MILITARY?

No. Attritable Swarm is an independent, fictional game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the U.S. Department of Defense, the U.S. Army, or any government agency.

WHAT DATA DO YOU COLLECT?

We do not require accounts or collect personal details. Advertising and analytics use cookies as described in the Privacy Policy.

HOW DO I REPORT A BUG OR SUGGEST AN IDEA?

Email attritable.swarm.game.contact@gmail.com or use the envelope button in the game, which opens your email app with a pre-addressed message.

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit attritableswarm.org (“Attritable Swarm”, “the site”, “the game”) and how it is used. The game requires no account and no sign-up.

INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do not ask for or store your name, email address, or any account details. We do not run our own user database or login system.

DATA STORED IN YOUR BROWSER

The game uses your browser’s local storage to save the following on your own device only. This data is never sent to our servers:

  • Your best score
  • Your sound mute preference
  • Your chosen callsign (a nickname you type in; do not enter personal information)

You can erase this data at any time by clearing your browser’s site data for attritableswarm.org.

ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADSENSE)

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and may use advertising cookies to personalize ads. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

ANALYTICS (GOOGLE ANALYTICS)

We use Google Analytics to understand how the game is used, such as visits, device and browser type, approximate location, and in-game events (game start, game over, level reached, score, share method). This data is collected by Google using cookies and similar technologies and is used in aggregate to improve the game. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

CONSENT (EEA, UK, AND SWITZERLAND)

Where required by law, visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a consent message before cookies are used for advertising and analytics. You may change or withdraw your consent at any time through that message or by clearing your browser cookies.

FEEDBACK EMAIL

The in-game comment button opens your device’s email app with a pre-addressed message to attritable.swarm.game.contact@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. If you email us, we receive your email address and message content, and use them only to respond and improve the game.

SHARING YOUR SCORE

The share buttons open third-party services (X/Twitter, Facebook, WhatsApp, Reddit) in a new tab only when you click them. Those services have their own privacy policies.

CHILDREN’S PRIVACY

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

THIRD-PARTY LINKS

The site may link to external websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last revised.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy: attritable.swarm.game.contact@gmail.com

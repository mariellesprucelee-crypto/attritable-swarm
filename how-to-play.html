FIELD MANUAL — HOW TO PLAY

CALLSIGN

Enter a callsign on the start screen (up to 16 characters). It is saved in your browser and shown on your shared score card.

MOVEMENT & FIRING

  • Move: Arrow keys or A/D (touch: the on-screen left/right buttons) — shifts your carrier between lanes.
  • Fire: Space bar or the RELEASE button fires on the target in your lane; clicking or tapping a target fires on it directly from any lane.
  • Drone Release slider: sets how many drones go out per release. The red "NEED" tick shows exactly how many are left to finish your hovered/current target.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS

  • P: pause / resume
  • H or ?: open / close the field manual
  • M: mute / unmute sound

ARSENAL

Your arsenal is finite and regenerates faster the longer you hold off deploying. Hit zero and the mission ends.

TARGETS

Targets can't be scared off — only drones remove them. Each has a spin-up delay before it starts firing back, then deals ongoing damage until destroyed. Threat rises: soldier < vehicle < vehicle convoy < enemy tank < weapons plant, with an attack helicopter boss on milestone levels (every 8th level).

ELITE UNITS

From level 15, rare elite targets can appear: Fighter Jet, Enemy UAS, and Tank Column. They have high health and hit hard, but pay out large rewards. Expect them more often as levels climb.

HIGH-VALUE TARGETS

During some battlefield events a lone High-Value Target (gold-ringed dignitary) appears. It has low health and fires back, but destroying it awards a large score bonus, so strike fast.

EFFICIENCY & STREAKS

A clean kill with little wasted overkill earns an EFFICIENCY bonus and builds a streak multiplier. Clearing a level heals some health and raises your arsenal cap — the bonus shrinks each level. Clearing a level quickly earns a Swift Victory bonus.

SUPPLY CRATES

Occasionally telegraph in (olive/gold ring, no threat) — fire on it, tap/click it, or drive your carrier into that lane to collect. They roll an arsenal refill, a health boost, or (at higher levels) an EMP that stuns every active target's weapons. Ignore one too long and it disappears.

BATTLEFIELD EVENTS

Random events can turn a level: incoming artillery strikes, jammed comms, a high-value target, or a swarm-effectiveness window.

RANKS & SCORE

Your score determines your command rank, from Second Lieutenant up to General of the Army. Your best score is saved in your browser.

SHARING YOUR SCORE

On the mission-end screen, press SHARE SCORE to post your result. On supported devices this opens your native share sheet with a score card image; otherwise you can share to X/Twitter, Facebook, WhatsApp, or Reddit, copy the text, or download the score card image.

PAUSE

Press P at any time to pause the mission. The game also pauses automatically if you switch tabs or windows.

    QUICK REFERENCE

    MOVE: ←/→ or A/D (touch: the left/right buttons) — shifts your carrier between lanes.
    FIRE: Space bar, tap/click a target, or the touch RELEASE button — releases the swarm on whatever's in your lane.
    DRONE RELEASE slider: sets how many drones go out per release. Watch the red "NEED" tick above the slider — it shows the exact count left to finish off your hovered/current target, so you can avoid overkill.

    ARSENAL is finite and regenerates faster the longer you hold off deploying. Hit zero and the mission ends.
    TARGETS can't be scared off — only drones remove them. Each has a spin-up delay before it starts firing back, then deals ongoing damage until destroyed. Threat rises: soldier < vehicle < vehicle convoy < enemy tank < weapons plant, with an attack helicopter boss on milestone levels.

    A clean kill with little wasted overkill earns an EFFICIENCY bonus and builds a streak multiplier. Clearing a level heals some health and raises your arsenal cap — the bonus shrinks each level.

    SUPPLY CRATES occasionally telegraph in (olive/gold ring, no threat) — fire on it, tap/click it, or just drive your carrier into that lane to collect. They roll an arsenal refill, a health boost, or (at higher levels) an EMP that stuns every active target's weapons. Ignore one too long and it disappears.

    P pauses the mission at any time.

    ATTRITABLE SWARM — Free Military Drone Swarm Browser Game

    Attritable Swarm is a free, no-download military-themed browser game. Command a mobile drone carrier along the forward line, deploy your drone swarm against soldiers, technicals, vehicle convoys, enemy tanks, weapons plants, and attack helicopters, and survive as many levels as you can. Play instantly in your browser on desktop or mobile — no install, no sign-up.

    HOW TO PLAY

    • Move your carrier between lanes with the arrow keys, A/D, or on-screen buttons.
    • Set your swarm size with the DRONE RELEASE slider, then release drones on the target in your lane.
    • Your arsenal is finite and regenerates over time — manage it carefully to survive.
    • Clear levels to heal up and raise your arsenal cap; watch for random battlefield events and supply crates.

ABOUT ATTRITABLE SWARM

Attritable Swarm is a free, browser-based military strategy arcade game. You command a mobile drone carrier, deploy a swarm of expendable ("attritable") drones against incoming threats — soldiers, technicals, vehicle convoys, enemy tanks, weapons plants, and attack helicopters — and try to survive as many levels as possible.

WHY "ATTRITABLE"?

The game draws its name and theme from modern attritable drone warfare doctrine: cheap, numerous, expendable drone systems used in large swarms rather than a handful of exquisite, high-cost platforms. Manage a finite, regenerating arsenal, balance swarm size against overkill, and clear the field before your carrier is overrun.

NO DOWNLOAD, NO SIGN-UP

The entire game runs in a single page in your browser — desktop or mobile. Nothing to install, no account required. Progress (your best score) is saved locally in your browser.
